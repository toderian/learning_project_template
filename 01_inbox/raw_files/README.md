# Raw Files

Temporary raw imports and pasted Obsidian attachments waiting for triage.

Move curated files into
`04_areas/<area>/assets/<images|attachments|imports>/<topic-path>/`
with timestamp-prefixed filenames.

Raw files and exports can expose credentials, personal data, client data,
account pages, or private context. Keep sensitive originals in `.no-commit/`
unless they are redacted and intentionally safe to commit.
